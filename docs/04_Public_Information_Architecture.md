# PawGuard Public Information Architecture

## Site Map

```
Home (/)
├── Mission Statement
├── Emergency Action
├── How PawGuard Works
├── Impact Metrics
├── Services (Emergency, Adoption, Vet, Foster)
├── Featured Animals
├── Volunteer / Donate CTA
├── Success Stories (featured)
├── FAQ
└── Final CTA

About (/about)
├── Mission & Vision
├── Our Story
├── Impact Stats
├── Values
├── Transparency
├── Team
└── Join CTA

Emergency (/emergency)
├── Hotline Banner
├── Severity Toggle (Critical / Non-Critical)
├── Report Form
│   ├── Location
│   ├── Animal Description
│   ├── Photo Upload
│   └── Contact Number
├── What Happens Next
├── Emergency Contacts
└── Safety Guidance

Adoption (/adopt)
├── Header
├── Filter Sidebar (Species, Age, Size)
├── Animal Grid (with sort)
├── Empty State
└── Adoption Process

Volunteer (/volunteer)
├── Hero
├── Stats Bar
├── Role Cards
├── Requirements
├── Process
└── Application Form

Donate (/donate)
├── Hero
├── Frequency Toggle
├── Amount Selector
├── Payment Demo
├── Impact Breakdown
├── Allocation Chart
└── Trust Badges

Stories (/stories)
├── Header
├── Featured Story
├── Story Grid
├── Share CTA
└── Back to Adoption

Contact (/contact)
├── Emergency Redirect
├── FAQ Accordion
├── Contact Form
└── Direct Contacts

404 (catch-all)
├── Error Display
└── Back to Home
```

## Navigation Hierarchy

### Primary (Navbar, all pages)
1. Home
2. Adopt
3. Volunteer
4. About
5. Stories
6. Contact

### Highlighted Actions (Navbar, always visible)
- Donate (primary button)
- Emergency (outlined button)

### Footer
- Services: Emergency Rescue | Animal Adoption | Volunteer | Donate
- Company: About Us | Success Stories | Contact | Privacy | Terms
- Newsletter signup

## Page Relationships
- Homepage links to all other pages
- Emergency page links to phone dialing
- Adoption page links to individual animal pages (future)
- Contact page links to Emergency page (redirect banner)
- Footer links to all major pages
