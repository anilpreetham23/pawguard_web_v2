# PawGuard Communication & Tone Guide

## Voice Foundation

PawGuard speaks as a **calm, competent coordinator** — experienced, direct, and genuinely warm. Every word should feel like it comes from someone who has done this work for years and knows exactly what to say in every situation.

## Tone by Mode

### URGENCY Mode
| Quality | Expression |
|---------|------------|
| Directive | "Stay where you are. Do not approach the animal." |
| Clear | "We need your location to dispatch a team." |
| Reassuring | "A team has been assigned. ETA: 12 minutes." |
| No sentiment | "Critical emergency detected. Immediate dispatch activated." |

**Avoid:** "We know this is a difficult time for you..." (delays the message)

### CONNECTION Mode
| Quality | Expression |
|---------|------------|
| Warm | "Meet Bella. She loves long walks and belly rubs." |
| Specific | "Bella is a 2-year-old Labrador Mix. She is fully vaccinated, spayed, and microchipped." |
| Honest | "Bella needs a home without small children or other pets." |
| Inviting | "Could Bella be your next companion?" |

**Avoid:** "This gorgeous girl is looking for her forever home!" (too sentimental)

### EVIDENCE Mode
| Quality | Expression |
|---------|------------|
| Specific | "78% of every dollar goes directly to animal care programs." |
| Verifiable | "View our 2024 audited financial statement (PDF, 2.4 MB)" |
| Transparent | "Administrative costs: 12%. Industry average: 18%." |
| Confident | "Your gift of $50 will fund a complete veterinary assessment for one rescued animal." |

**Avoid:** "Your generous contribution will make a real difference!" (vague)

### COMMUNITY Mode
| Quality | Expression |
|---------|------------|
| Personal | "Maria drives rescue animals 200 miles every weekend. She started as a foster." |
| Clear | "Commitment: 4 hours per week. Training provided." |
| Welcoming | "No experience required. We'll teach you everything." |
| Purposeful | "Every hour you give directly saves an animal's life." |

**Avoid:** "Join our amazing team of dedicated volunteers!" (generic)

### NARRATIVE Mode
| Quality | Expression |
|---------|------------|
| Journalistic | "Bruno was found severely malnourished and unable to walk following a road accident near the Millbrook interchange." |
| Specific | "PawGuard's emergency team reached the scene in under 12 minutes." |
| Emotional | "After six weeks of intensive care, Bruno was adopted by the Lindqvist family." |
| Real | Names, dates, locations — never anonymized. |

**Avoid:** "One brave dog's journey from hardship to happiness." (cliched)

## Microcopy System

### CTAs

| Context | Bad | Good |
|---------|-----|------|
| Emergency report | "Submit" | "Send Rescue Request" |
| Adoption | "Learn More" | "Meet Bella" (animal name) |
| Donation | "Donate Now" | "Protect an Animal Today" |
| Volunteer | "Apply" | "Join the Rescue Team" |
| Contact | "Send" | "Send Message" |
| Newsletter | "Subscribe" | "Get Rescue Updates" |
| General | "Click Here" | [never use "Click Here"] |

### Form Labels

| Field | Bad | Good |
|-------|-----|------|
| Name | "Full Name" | "Your Name" |
| Email | "Email Address" | "Your Email" |
| Message | "Message" | "How can we help?" |
| Location | "Location" | "Where is the animal?" |
| Description | "Description" | "Describe the situation" |
| Photo | "Upload Photo" | "Add a photo (optional)" |

### Success States

| Action | Bad | Good |
|--------|-----|------|
| Emergency report | "Form submitted" | "Your report has reached our rescue team. A unit is being dispatched." |
| Donation | "Payment successful" | "Your gift of $50 will fund a full veterinary assessment. Thank you." |
| Volunteer app | "Application received" | "Welcome to PawGuard. We'll contact you within 5 business days." |
| Contact form | "Message sent" | "Thank you. We typically respond within 1-2 business days." |

### Error States

| Context | Bad | Good |
|---------|-----|------|
| Location missing | "Location is required" | "We need the animal's location to send help" |
| Invalid email | "Invalid email" | "Please enter a valid email so we can reach you" |
| General error | "Something went wrong" | "We couldn't submit your report. Please check your connection and try again." |

### Loading States

| Context | Bad | Good |
|---------|-----|------|
| Emergency submit | "Submitting..." | "Contacting nearest rescue unit..." |
| Donation | "Processing..." | "Securely processing your payment..." |
| Volunteer app | "Submitting..." | "Sending your application..." |

## Writing Rules

1. **One sentence per point.** No compound sentences in emergency content.
2. **Numbers before adjectives.** "4,200 rescues" not "thousands of rescues."
3. **Active voice always.** "We rescued" not "A rescue was performed."
4. **You/We language.** "Your report. Our team. We respond." — builds relationship.
5. **No exclamation marks in emergency.** In narrative and connection modes, limited use.
6. **No rhetorical questions.** "Could you be the one to make a difference?" — removed.
7. **No guilt.** Never imply the user should have acted sooner or given more.
8. **Be specific about time.** "within 24 hours" → same day. "soon" → within minutes.
9. **Write for scanning.** Short paragraphs. Bold key terms. Bullet lists.
10. **Read everything aloud.** If it sounds unnatural spoken, rewrite it.
