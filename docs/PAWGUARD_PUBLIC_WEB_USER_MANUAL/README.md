# PawGuard Public Web — End User Manual

Welcome to the official End User Manual for the **PawGuard Public Web Application**. This manual provides comprehensive, step-by-step guidance for pet owners, animal lovers, finders, and community members using PawGuard to protect, adopt, rescue, and care for animals.

---

## About PawGuard

PawGuard is an end-to-end digital platform dedicated to animal welfare, pet safety, and community rescue. The Public Web application provides accessible tools to ensure every companion and stray animal receives timely care, shelter, and protection.

---

## Purpose of the Website

The PawGuard Public Web application empowers users to:
* **Protect Companion Pets:** Register pets, generate digital QR Safety Tags, and track health schedules.
* **Reunite Lost Pets:** Report lost pets, post found animal reports, and submit location sightings.
* **Adopt Animals:** Browse rescue animals, submit adoption applications, and track application progress.
* **Emergency Rescue:** Submit urgent rescue requests for injured or trapped animals with precise GPS coordinates.
* **Access Veterinary Care:** Discover partner vet clinics, review services, and book appointments online.
* **Support Animal Care:** Make secure, tax-deductible donations to support ongoing animal rescue operations.

---

## Website Accessibility & Account Requirements

### What You Can Do Without an Account (Public Browsing)
* Browse the home page, community success stories, and educational articles.
* Search for partner veterinary clinics and view services.
* Scan a PawGuard QR Safety Tag on a found pet to view public safety notes and report a sighting.
* Report an injured animal emergency with GPS coordinates.
* Browse adoptable animals and read community success stories.
* Make a donation via Razorpay.

### What Requires a Free PawGuard Account (Signed-In Features)
* Registering and managing your companion pets.
* Generating and linking QR Safety Tags to your pets.
* Setting pet health reminders (vaccinations, deworming, vet visits).
* Booking and managing veterinary appointments.
* Submitting adoption applications and tracking progress.
* Managing your profile, saved pets, and viewing donation receipts.
* Receiving real-time account notifications.

## Navigation Overview

The website header provides instant access to all core sections:

* **Home (`/`):** Live rescue ticker, urgent community alerts, quick actions, and impact statistics.
* **Adopt (`/adopt`):** Explore adoptable dogs and cats looking for loving homes.
* **Lost & Found (`/lost-found`):** Browse lost/found listings and submit reports.
* **Veterinary (`/veterinary`):** Find partner clinics and book health checkups.
* **Emergency (`/emergency`):** Submit urgent rescue requests for animals in distress.
* **Donate (`/donate`):** Contribute to animal food, medical care, and rescue funds.
* **User Account Menu:** Access My Account (`/account`), My Pets (`/account/pets`), Applications (`/applications`), Reminders (`/reminders`), and Notifications (`/notifications`).

## Quick-Start Paths

* **Found a lost pet with a QR tag?** Scan the tag using your phone camera or visit `/scan` to report a sighting.
* **Pet went missing?** Go to [Lost & Found](08-lost-and-found/README.md) and click **Report Lost Pet**.
* **Spotted an injured stray?** Go to [Emergency Rescue](12-emergency-and-urgent-alerts/README.md) and submit a GPS location report immediately.
* **Want to adopt a pet?** Visit [Adoption Catalog](11-adoption-and-rescue/README.md) to explore pets seeking homes.
